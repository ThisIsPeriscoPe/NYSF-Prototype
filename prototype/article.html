Alumni story

From a regional town to a research career

By Ayla Connolly· 18 June 2026· 5 min read

When Maya boarded the bus from her regional town, she'd never met another student as obsessed with chemistry as she was. Nine days later, she left NYSF with a notebook full of contacts, a head full of ideas and, for the first time, a clear sense that a career in science was actually possible.

A turning point

For students far from a major city, the path into STEM can feel abstract. NYSF exists to make it concrete: real labs, real researchers, and a community of peers who get it.

"I arrived thinking I might like science. I left certain. That certainty changed every decision I made afterwards."

Finding her people

The friendships made at NYSF often last well beyond the program. For Maya, they became a support network through university and into her first research role.

Paying it forward

Today, Maya mentors current participants, closing the loop for the next student staring out a bus window, wondering if science is for them. (It is.)
