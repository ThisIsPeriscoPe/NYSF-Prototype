What is NYSF

More than a program: a launchpad

The National Youth Science Forum is a not-for-profit that connects young people with the people, places and possibilities of STEM. Through residential programs, STEM Hubs and the National Youth STEM Summit, students meet working scientists, tour real labs, and discover where their curiosity can lead.

  • ✓ Run by youth, for youth, for over 40 years
  • ✓ Hands-on access to universities, industry and research
  • ✓ Scholarships and access & equity support for every background
