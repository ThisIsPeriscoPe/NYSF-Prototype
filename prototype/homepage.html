For curious young Australians

See where science could take you

For more than 40 years, NYSF has helped young Australians explore STEM beyond the classroom. We believe every young person should have the opportunity to discover where STEM can take them through immersive programs, inspiring connections and experiences that build confidence, broaden horizons and open doors to future possibilities.
