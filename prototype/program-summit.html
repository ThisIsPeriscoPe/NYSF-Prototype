National Youth STEM Summit

Three days to shape your future STEM career.

Register now Explore other programs

About the program

The National Youth STEM Summit is a three-day, conference-style event for people aged 18 and over who are studying or starting out in STEM. In 2026, it runs in Canberra from 12 to 14 September, themed “Shaping Your STEM Career.” Expect keynotes from leading scientists, panels, skill-building workshops, and networking with peers and professionals.

What’s included

  • ✓ Keynotes from leading STEM professionals
  • ✓ Networking with peers and mentors
  • ✓ Showcases and panels
  • ✓ A national community

Who it’s for

People aged 18 and over in tertiary study or training, or in the early stages of their careers. Others are welcome if they feel it would benefit them.

Common questions

When and where? +
12 to 14 September 2026, at the ANU Research School of Physics, University of Canberra and the Australian Parliament House.
Can I attend if I’m not 18 yet? +
The National Youth STEM Summit is strictly for over-18s. If you are under 18, you can explore our other programs.
How do I register? +
Registrations are now open via Eventbrite here. Sign up to be notified.
Is there a cost involved? +
Tickets for the National Youth STEM Summit are $250, but please get in touch at nysf@nysf.edu.au if there are any barriers.

What to expect

Three days, three themes

Day 1 · Vision

Looking forward: what the future may hold for attendees in their chosen STEM fields.

  • 9.30amArrival
  • 11.00amOpening Keynote
  • 12.00pmNetworking Lunch
  • 1.00pmWho Decides the Future of STEM? Policy, Ethics & Innovation
  • 2.00pmCareers that Don’t Exist Yet, in conversation with Australia’s Chief Scientist, Professor Tony Haymet
  • 3.00pmAfternoon Tea
  • 3.30pmWhere Will Science & Technology Take Us?

Day 2 · Momentum

The skills attendees will need to succeed in their future STEM careers.

  • 10.00amDay 2 Keynote
  • 11.00amBeyond the Textbook: skills no one teaches you
  • 12.00pmNetworking Lunch
  • 1.00pmWorkshop: Polishing Your Presence, delivered by Dr Fiona Scotney
  • 3.00pmAfternoon Tea
  • 3.30pmSession to be announced

Day 3 · Pathways

The pathways open to attendees, culminating in a Parliamentary Networking Event in the Mural Hall of Australian Parliament House.

  • 10.00amThe Scenic Route: Non-Linear Careers & Opportunities You Didn’t See Coming

  • 12.00pmNetworking Lunch
  • 1.00pmSTEM Study and Careers Expo
  • 3.00pmAfternoon Tea
  • 3.30pmClosing Keynote
