How to get involved

Every young Australian who reaches NYSF does so because someone made it possible.

For supporters

Your support reaches students who need it most

Whether you give, volunteer your time, advocate for our programs, or partner your organisation with us, your support reaches the students who need it most.

Donations are channelled straight into scholarships and keeping our programs affordable, so a student’s postcode or family income never decides whether they get to take part.

  • ✓ Scholarships for students who need them
  • ✓ Mentorship and leadership opportunities
  • ✓ STEM Hubs reaching regional Australia
  • ✓ Programs kept affordable for all
  • ✓ A direct, measurable community impact
