Partners & opportunities

How organisations partner with NYSF to build Australia's STEM workforce pipeline.

Why partner

A direct line to future STEM talent

NYSF reaches thousands of high-potential students each year, right as they choose their path. Partners help shape that path, and build visibility, goodwill and a pipeline that pays off for decades.

  • ✓ Connect with motivated young people early
  • ✓ Demonstrate genuine STEM commitment
  • ✓ Reach regional and under-represented students
