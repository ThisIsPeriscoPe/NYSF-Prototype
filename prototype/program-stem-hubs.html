NYSF STEM Hubs

Hands-on STEM experiences, closer to home.

Apply now Download brochure

About the program

NYSF STEM Hubs bring hands-on science to students in regional and metropolitan areas across Australia, running throughout the year. Shorter, local and accessible, often a student’s first taste of NYSF.

What's included

  • ✓ Local day or multi-day workshops
  • ✓ Hands-on activities with mentors
  • ✓ Connection to the national NYSF network
  • ✓ A pathway toward the Year 12 Program
  • ✓ Run in partnership with local hosts

Who it's for

Open to Year 9–12 students across Australia, with a focus on reaching regional and remote communities.

Common questions

Do I need top marks to take part? +
No. Selection is based on curiosity and motivation, not just academic results.
Is financial support available? +
Yes. Scholarships and access & equity support help make NYSF accessible to every student.
Where does it take place? +
Across NYSF's partner universities and venues around Australia.
