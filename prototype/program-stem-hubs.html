About the program

NYSF STEM Hubs are shorter, non-residential experiences for students in Years 10 to 12. You tour science and technology facilities, meet researchers and industry professionals, explore university and career pathways, and connect with other curious students.

What’s included

  • ✓ Facility tours
  • ✓ Transport on program
  • ✓ Meet researchers, academics and industry
  • ✓ Explore university and career pathways
  • ✓ Connect with like-minded students
  • ✓ Build confidence in your next steps

Who it’s for

Students in Years 10, 11 or 12 with an interest in STEM.

Common questions

What does it cost? +
The standard ticket is $80, covering all programmed visits, activities and daily program transport. Travel to the host city and accommodation aren’t included.
Is support available? +
Yes. Reduced rates apply for NYSF alumni, school groups of more than five, and under the Access and Equity Scholarship program. Contact nysf@nysf.edu.au.
Where and when? +
Hubs run in different cities through the year. The 2026 Sydney STEM Hub runs 7 to 9 July, with visits to UNSW, UTS, Macquarie University, Atlassian, ADF Careers and more.
