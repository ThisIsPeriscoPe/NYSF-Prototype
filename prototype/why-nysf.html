For educators

Why educators recommend NYSF

NYSF gives your most curious students experiences you can’t run alone: real labs, working scientists and a national peer network. For many it’s the moment a vague interest in science becomes a clear direction.

It’s also one of the easiest high-impact things you can offer. We handle the program; you simply point a student our way, with materials and evidence ready for your leadership team.

  • ✓ Curriculum-aligned, genuine benefit
  • ✓ Simple to recommend or host
  • ✓ Evidence to share with leadership
  • ✓ Reaches students you might otherwise miss

For partners

Why partner with us

Partnering with NYSF connects your organisation to the next generation of scientists and engineers, early, authentically, and with measurable community impact you can report against.

We work with universities, government and industry to shape partnerships around your goals, whether that’s talent, regional reach, or a visible commitment to STEM and equity.

  • ✓ Reach high-potential talent early
  • ✓ Genuine STEM & community commitment
  • ✓ Clear reporting and recognition
  • ✓ Flexible models, from headline to in-kind

For alumni

Why alumni stay connected

Alumni are the heart of NYSF. Staying connected keeps you close to a national network, and lets you mentor, lead and give back to the next generation of curious young Australians.

The relationships made over twelve days have a way of lasting decades. Alumni tell us their cohort became a professional network, a friendship group, and a reason to keep showing up for the next round of students.

  • ✓ Mentor and inspire current students
  • ✓ Events and a national network
  • ✓ Opportunities to lead and volunteer
  • ✓ Lifelong friendships and contacts
