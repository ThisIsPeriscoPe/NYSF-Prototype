Why NYSF

What makes NYSF different, and why educators, partners and alumni stay connected.

For educators

Why educators recommend NYSF

NYSF changes what a young person thinks is possible for them. Participants come away with a clearer sense of direction, a group of peers from around the country, and time spent in real research settings that a classroom can’t offer. Selection isn’t based on marks alone, and scholarships mean cost doesn’t filter students out before they start.

  • ✓ Curriculum-aligned, genuine benefit
  • ✓ Simple to recommend or host
  • ✓ Selection on curiosity, not just marks

For partners

Why partner with us

Partnering with NYSF puts your organisation in front of motivated young people right when they’re choosing their direction in STEM. You meet emerging talent early, show a real commitment to the future of Australian science, and help shape an experience that thousands of students carry with them. Partnership is flexible, from funding and scholarships through to hosting visits and providing mentors.

  • ✓ Meet emerging talent early
  • ✓ Flexible: funding, scholarships, visits, mentors
  • ✓ A real commitment to Australian science
Partner with NYSF
For alumni

Why stay connected

Alumni are at the heart of NYSF. Staying connected keeps you in a national network of people building careers in STEM, and it’s how you can help the next group through, whether that’s leading on the Student Staff Leadership Program or mentoring someone younger.

  • ✓ A national network of people building STEM careers
  • ✓ Lead through the Student Staff Leadership Program
  • ✓ Mentor someone younger
