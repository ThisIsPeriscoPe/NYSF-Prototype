Access & Equity Scholarships

Talent is everywhere; opportunity isn’t. Our scholarships make sure cost is never the reason a curious student misses out.

Access and Equity

STEM is for everyone

Through scholarships and the support of partners like Rotary, we help young people from every background and postcode take part in NYSF. We are committed to ensuring that as many talented young Australians can participate in our programs as possible.

How to apply
The detail

How it works

Who it’s for

Assessed on circumstances including living in a remote or regional area, a low socio-economic background, being Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander, or any other background that might need support.

How it works

The scholarship is a discount on program fees, sized to your circumstances. It can be combined with other funding, including Rotary, which helps students who meet more than one criterion.

How to apply

Start your Year 12 Program application and complete the Access and Equity Scholarship section. It’s assessed alongside your application, with a letter of support from a responsible person outside your family.
