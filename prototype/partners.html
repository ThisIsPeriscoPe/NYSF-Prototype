For partners and organisations

Support the next generation of STEM talent

Every NYSF program runs on partnership. Universities open their labs, industry opens its doors, and organisations back the young people who’ll shape their fields. Partnering puts you in front of motivated students right as they choose what to study and where to work.

Why NYSF

Invest in Australia's future STEM talent

By partnering with NYSF, your organisation can help inspire and support the next generation of STEM professionals while building meaningful connections with talented young people from across Australia. Whether you're looking to strengthen your community impact, engage future talent or contribute to STEM education, we'll work with you to create a partnership that delivers shared value.
