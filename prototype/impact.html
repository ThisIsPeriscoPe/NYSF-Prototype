Outcomes

Curiosity that lasts a lifetime

NYSF participants don't just enjoy the experience; they go on to study, research and lead in STEM at far higher rates than the national average. Many return as mentors, closing the loop for the next generation.

Access & Equity

Science is for everyone

Talent is everywhere; opportunity isn't. Through scholarships, our Access & Equity program and the support of partners like Rotary, we make sure a young person's postcode or background never decides their future in science.
