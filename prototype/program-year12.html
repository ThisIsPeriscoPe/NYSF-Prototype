Year 12 Program

Nine days that can change the course of your future in STEM.

Apply now Scholarship support

About the program

The Year 12 Program is NYSF’s flagship. Over nine days on a university campus you tour science and technology facilities, learn about current research, meet industry partners, explore university and STEM career pathways, and join students from all over Australia. In January 2027 it runs two sessions: the Australian National University in Canberra from 6 to 14 January, and the University of Queensland in Brisbane from 13 to 21 January.

What’s included

  • ✓ Nine days of accommodation and meals
  • ✓ Hands-on lab and research sessions
  • ✓ University and industry site visits
  • ✓ Talks from working scientists
  • ✓ A national peer network
  • ✓ Social and wellbeing program

Who it’s for

Open to students in Year 11 about to start Year 12, from every background and every part of Australia. Selection is based on curiosity and motivation, not just academic results, and Access and Equity Scholarships mean cost need not be a barrier.

Read more about the application process here.

Common questions

Do I need top marks? +
No. Selection is based on curiosity and motivation, not just results.
Is financial support available? +
Yes. Access and Equity Scholarships, plus support from Rotary, help make it accessible.
How long is it, and where? +
Nine days, in January 2027. Two sessions: Canberra, 6 to 14 January, and Brisbane, 13 to 21 January.
What does it cost? +
The 2027 program fee is $3,180, plus a $70 application fee. The fee covers accommodation, transport on session, meals and activities, but not travel to or from the program.
