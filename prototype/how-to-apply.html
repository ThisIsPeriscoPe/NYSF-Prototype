How to apply

Everything you need to apply for the 2027 NYSF Year 12 Program.

Before you start

Check your eligibility

Selection isn’t based solely on academic results, and there’s no limit on how many students can apply from one school or area.

  • ✓ You’re currently a Year 11 student, applying for the 2027 Year 12 Program
  • ✓ You’re thinking about study, training or work in STEM or STEM-related fields
  • ✓ You’re an Australian citizen or resident, and/or studying full-time in an Australian high school

International or overseas? Contact nysf@nysf.edu.au to express your interest. If you’d like us to be aware of any special considerations or need reasonable adjustments, get in touch for confidential support.

We welcome everyone

Applications are encouraged from students

Remote or regional areas

Wherever you live, NYSF is designed to reach you.

Out-of-home care

Living arrangements should never limit curiosity.

Low socio-economic background

Scholarships mean cost need not be a barrier.

Disability or chronic illness

Reasonable adjustments are available; just ask.

Six steps

How the application works

1

Start an online application

Applications for the 2027 program are open and close Thursday, 23 July 2026.

2

Complete all questions

Motivation, academic record, future plans, extracurriculars and more; see the detail below.

3

Apply for a scholarship

Complete the Access and Equity Scholarship section if your circumstances affect your ability to attend.

4

Pay the fee and submit

A non-refundable $70 application fee; you’ll receive a confirmation email.

5

Your application is reviewed

The NYSF Selection Panel reviews all applications; outcomes advised in August 2026.

6

If successful, accept your place

The 2027 program fee is $3,180, invoiced on acceptance and due October 2026.

The detail

What the application asks

Motivation & interest +
Why do you want to attend the NYSF Year 12 Program?
Academic record +
Comment on your recent results: subjects, usual grades, and any extenuating circumstances we should be mindful of. NYSF encourages a broad range of participants from diverse academic backgrounds.
Your future plans +
What are your plans for after Year 12? Further education, a gap year, work, or something else? If you’re not sure yet, that’s okay; just write about your current intentions.
Extracurricular activities +
Outline activities you take part in outside school. If work or other commitments such as caring responsibilities limit these, tell us here.
Value of attendance +
What do you think you’ll get out of the program, and what can you add to the experience for other participants?
School report +
Upload your most recent school report. If you haven’t received a Year 11 report yet, upload your most recent Year 10 report; don’t wait.
Your STEM areas of interest +
This helps shape the overall program. You won’t be grouped by these interests; everyone explores a wide range of STEM fields.
Session preference +
In 2027 the program runs in Canberra (6–14 January) and Brisbane (13–21 January). Tell us your preference; we do our best, though capacity means it’s not always possible. Travel to and from the program is the participant’s responsibility.
Financial support

Never let cost decide

Access and Equity Scholarships are funded by NYSF partners and the NYSF community to support students whose circumstances affect their ability to attend.

We also encourage students to seek support from NYSF’s founding partner, Rotary. Many Rotary clubs are familiar with NYSF and can offer funding or help students fundraise to attend. Details on accessing assistance are sent to all applicants at key stages.
