For students

How to apply

Applications for the Year 12 Program open mid-year and close in spring. Selection is based on your curiosity and motivation, not just your marks, and scholarships mean cost is never a barrier to taking part.

You can apply yourself, or ask a teacher to put your name forward. The form takes about twenty minutes, and our team is on hand if you get stuck.

  • ✓ Open to Year 11 students moving into Year 12
  • ✓ 12 days of labs, talks and site visits
  • ✓ Accommodation, meals and a national peer network
  • ✓ Scholarships and access & equity support

Application questions

The things students ask most

Do I need top marks to take part? +
No. Selection is based on curiosity and motivation, not just academic results.
What does it cost, and what if we can’t afford it? +
Fees apply, but scholarships and access & equity support mean cost is never a barrier. Just ask.
When do applications open? +
Mid-year, closing in spring. Join NYSF Outlook to be told the moment dates are confirmed.
Can my teacher apply on my behalf? +
Yes. Teachers can nominate a student, and we’ll guide you through the rest.
For families

How to support your child

You don’t need a science background to help your teenager take this step. The most valuable thing you can do is encourage them, help them meet the deadline, and reassure them that cost won’t stand in the way.

NYSF is fully supervised by trained staff, with a wellbeing program running alongside the science. For most families, the hardest part is simply deciding to apply; everything after that, we help with.

  • ✓ No science background needed from you
  • ✓ Trained staff and a full wellbeing program
  • ✓ Scholarships for families who need them
  • ✓ Guidance on travel and what to pack

For educators

Educators

Teachers are how most students first hear about NYSF. Whether you recommend one curious student or bring a whole STEM Hub to your region, we make it simple, and we give you the evidence to take to school leadership.

You don’t need to run anything yourself. We provide ready-made materials, a named contact, and the research to show the genuine, curriculum-aligned benefit for your students.

  • ✓ Ready-made posters and assembly slides
  • ✓ Curriculum-aligned and evidence-backed
  • ✓ A named NYSF contact to support you
  • ✓ Options from a single nomination to a full Hub

For alumni

Alumni

NYSF doesn’t end when the program does. Thousands of alumni stay involved, mentoring current students, speaking at events, and stepping up through the Student Leadership Program to help run NYSF for the next cohort.

Staying connected keeps you close to a national network that spans every field of science and every state. Many alumni say the contacts and friendships they made are still among their most valuable, years later.

  • ✓ Lifelong friendships and contacts
  • ✓ Early news on events and roles
  • ✓ Mentoring and speaking opportunities
  • ✓ A platform to give back and lead

For supporters

Supporters

Every young Australian who reaches NYSF does so because someone made it possible. Whether you give, volunteer your time, advocate for STEM, or partner your organisation with us, your support reaches the students who need it most.

Donations are channelled straight into scholarships, regional STEM Hubs and keeping our programs affordable, so a student’s postcode or family income never decides whether they get to take part.

  • ✓ Scholarships for students who need them
  • ✓ STEM Hubs reaching regional Australia
  • ✓ Programs kept affordable for all
  • ✓ A direct, measurable community impact
