Why NYSF

A trusted partner for your classroom

NYSF complements the curriculum with experiences you can’t run alone: real labs, working scientists and a national peer network. Recommending NYSF is one of the highest-impact things you can do for a STEM-curious student.

  • ✓ Curriculum-aligned, genuine student benefit
  • ✓ Simple to recommend, promote or host
  • ✓ Evidence & testimonials to share with leadership
