FAQs

Your guide to common questions

Who can apply to NYSF programs? +
Programs are open to students in Years 11 and 12 with a curiosity for science, technology, engineering and maths.
How long do the programs run? +
Formats vary from short online courses to the flagship twelve-day residential experience.
Is financial support available? +
Yes — a range of scholarships and partner-funded places help make programs accessible to every young Australian.
Do I need top marks to be selected? +
No. Selection is based on curiosity and motivation, not just academic results.
